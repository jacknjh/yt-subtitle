What do these buttons do?

Load transcript JSON reads the words and timings for the active source sequence. Play preview and the timing slider preview the selected phrase without creating clips.


Generate subtitles creates a separate sequence with editable word graphics. Stop keeps the completed graphics after the current batch.


Set parent from playhead remembers that graphic. Sync selected to parent copies its current appearance to selected graphics, preserving their words and timing. This is an explicit sync, not a continuous link.


Read selected phrase loads the full phrase containing your selected generated graphic. Update phrase text corrects all its word graphics while retaining their current timeline positions.
