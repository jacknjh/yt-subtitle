Using the generator




Load transcript JSON
Reads words and their timings for the active source sequence. First read and save its JSON using the UXP transcript panel.


Style and preview
Choose a phrase, play or scrub its timing, then adjust appearance. The preview is approximate and does not modify timeline graphics.


Fonts and colors
Search local font faces, then choose one from the dropdown. Click a color swatch to open its picker, or type a six-digit hex color. New fonts appear after rerunning the panel installer.


Generate subtitles
Creates a duplicate sequence with editable word graphics on an empty top video track. Your source sequence is preserved.


Stop
Finishes the current batch and keeps completed graphics. The progress bar shows completed graphics and elapsed time.


Set parent / Sync selected
Remembers the generated graphic under the playhead, then copies its latest appearance to the selected destinations. Press Sync again after changing the parent.


Read / Update phrase
Loads the phrase associated with a selected generated graphic. Update rewrites all its word graphics while retaining their timeline positions. Duplicate or delete word graphics first when changing the word count.
